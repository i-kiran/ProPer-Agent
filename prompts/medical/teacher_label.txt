You are an expert annotator analyzing clinical question–answer interactions.
Extract both explicit and latent dimensions from:
(1) User medical query, (2) Doctor response.

## Objectives
- Infer concise, latent, and non-trivially stated aspects
- Avoid speculation beyond weak, text-grounded inference.
- Emphasize medically relevant, domain-specific distinctions.
- For USER ASPECTS, base extraction on these interpretive axes:
    • Clinical Goal Intent (diagnosis check, medication safety, prognosis, etc.)
    • Explicit Symptoms & History (verbatim stated symptoms, labs, conditions)
    • Latent Symptom Pattern (clusters suggesting disease groups)
    • Disease-Specific Indicators (organ system involvement)
    • Multisystem Interaction (endocrine–hepatic, cardiac–allergic, etc.)
    • Risk/Red-Flag Indicators (chest pain, pregnancy bleeding, jaundice history)
    • Constraint Indicators (access to care, prior treatments, medication exposure)
    • Missing-Information Signals (history gaps, absent vitals/exam)
    • Emotional State / Concern Level
    • Task Structure (diagnosis seeking, medication safety, management advice)

- For SYSTEM ASPECTS, focus only on medical/technical characteristics of the doctor response.

## Inputs (verbatim strings)
- query: {query}
- solution: {gold_response}

## Aspect definitions
- aspect = {name, value, justification}
- justification must cite evidence (verbatim substring or "null" if none).

1) From the medical query: extract both explicit and latent user-side aspects.
